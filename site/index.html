GET STARTED

Listen or set up a host.

Open your listening room.

Scan the QR code shown by your church, or paste the listening link from the booth. Choose Español or 한국어, then tap Listen live.

For a local church host, connect to the same church Wi-Fi.

Listening from a different computer or phone?

Any device with a modern browser can join an active host. You don’t need to install the AI models. A local listening link works on the host’s network; listening from outside that network needs a reachable hosted connection.

Set up the church host.

One church Mac processes the sermon. Listeners join from their own computers or phones.

  1. DOWNLOAD
    Download the host app

    Apple Silicon Mac · 16 GB memory minimum · 24 GB recommended · about 25 GB free storage.

    Download Gather Host for Mac ↓

    Windows, Linux, Intel Mac, phones, and tablets can join an existing host. Their native AI host builds are not available yet.

    If macOS asks about opening the app

    Unzip the download and move Gather Host to Applications. Open it. This first release is not notarized; if macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway for Gather Host. You can inspect the source and published checksum on GitHub.

  2. SET UP
    Open the app and download the models.

    Browser setup checks this Mac, shows download progress, and lets you include live sermon notes. Download the models once; Gather keeps them for future services.

    About 10.2 GB of core models, or 12.5 GB with sermon notes. First setup needs internet; models stay on this computer.

  3. GO LIVE
    Connect the sermon and share the link.

    Choose the sound desk, microphone, or livestream in the booth. Start the service and share its QR code. Keep the host app running.

    Open installed Gather ↗

    Already have Gather running? Open this computer’s booth.

NAMES & EXPRESSIONS

Church vocabulary.

Preserve a series title like “Peopling,” then explain its church-specific meaning once. Sermon context guides later translation.

SEARCH & DOWNLOAD

Live sermon notes.

Follow main points and supporting ideas as the message unfolds. Search their original transcript passages, then download the notes after the service.

HOST & LISTENERS

One local host.

The website is hosted by GitHub. AI processing happens on your church computer. A clean sound desk feed avoids a livestream platform’s added delay.

Working prototype. AI recognition and translation can make mistakes; accuracy and live latency depend on the source audio and host hardware.
